🤝 Hands‑On Volunteering

Working Volunteer

Roll up your sleeves and create real impact — join our field teams, community drives, and skill‑building programs.

What You Can Do

Working Volunteer Opportunities

Choose from a range of hands‑on roles that suit your skills and schedule.

🏘️

Community Outreach

Assist in organising health camps, literacy drives, and social welfare events in rural and urban areas. Join our C‑Force team for weekend drives.

🌳

Environmental Projects

Participate in tree‑planting campaigns, neighbourhood clean‑up drives, and sustainability awareness workshops with local schools.

💡

Skill‑Based Mentoring

Provide career guidance, computer literacy training, or entrepreneurship mentorship to youth and self‑help group members.

🎉

Event Management

Help plan and execute cultural festivals, fundraising galas, and awareness campaigns — from logistics to on‑the‑day coordination.

🍱

Food Distribution

Support the weekly Annam (food) programme by helping prepare, pack, and distribute nutritious meals to underprivileged households.

🧵

Vocational Training Support

Assist trainers during tailoring and handicraft classes for women, providing hands‑on guidance and quality checks.

Ready to Volunteer?

Send us a brief introduction — your name, location, skills, and preferred area of volunteering — and we'll match you with the right opportunity.
