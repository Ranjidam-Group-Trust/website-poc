🎨 Holistic Child & Community Initiatives

General Projects Overview

From skill building and creative expression to nutrition, excursions, and women's empowerment, explore RGT's 13 flagship general initiatives.

👩

1. Magalir Mattum

"There is no tool for development more effective than the empowerment of women."
Dedicated purely to women's empowerment, encouraging young women to step out of their comfort zone. We organize lectures on girl education, skill enhancement, health, hygiene, and self-defense against harassment, providing books and essential skill kits.

🎨

2. Varnangal

Children draw to express what they feel and think. To boost imagination, cognitive skills, and fine motor coordination, we host themed drawing and essay-writing competitions. Winners are awarded prizes to inspire their creative and competitive spirit.

💡

3. Gnanam

Interactive seminars for children to cultivate resilience, innovation, empathy, optimism, and life skills. Includes career-guidance programs led by trained volunteers to help students choose meaningful future pathways.

📖

4. Iragugal

Customized one-to-one tutoring provided by talented volunteers in flexible timings. Special focus on Spoken English, Grammar, Hindi, Academic Maths, and Computer Science to help timid students build confidence.

🚌

5. Payanam

Educational excursions and one-day trips in comfortable buses for orphanage children and home incharges. Complete with healthy snacks and meals, giving children memorable exposure away from routine life.

🍱

6. Ubasaripu

Donor-sponsored celebration meals for orphanage children during birthdays and anniversaries. Offered in 3 categories: Budget Meal, Exotic Meal (starters, main course, dessert), and Customized Meal.

🎁

7. Smile Over Miles

Fulfilling the specific wishes of underprivileged children. We take note of each child's long-cherished wish (stationary, toys, accessories, books) and fulfill it with tailored gifts, cultural performances, and festive meals.

🧘‍♂️

8. Yoga Camp

Regular yoga sessions conducted by trained professionals to promote physical strength, mental clarity, emotional focus, and perseverance among children.

🌾

9. Pongal - O - Pongal

Celebrating traditional 'Samathuva Pongal' with orphanage children. Includes cooking meals together, Rangoli contests, traditional games like Uriyadi and Kabadi, and tractor/bullock cart rides.

🥗

10. Puratham (P++)

Targeted nutrition and protein supply program ensuring children receive the required 19–32 grams of daily protein and essential micronutrients according to age and season.

🤝

11. Hands for Humanity (H4H)

Volunteer meal-serving drives where passionate volunteers and donors visit orphanages to personally prepare and serve warm food, spreading kindness and love.

🪔

12. Deepavali Oligal

Diwali celebrations with children in outlying orphanages. Providing new clothes, sweets, firecrackers, and full-day festive meals to bring joy to underserved communities.

🏃‍♂️

13. RWR & Manavar Kalam

RWR: Mini marathons for kids and volunteers to promote physical health and social causes.
Manavar Kalam: Practical civic awareness and youth engagement programs.
