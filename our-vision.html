🌟 Guiding Principles & Quality Policy

Our Vision & Mission

Fostering comprehensive national development through widespread, accessible, and impactful education and social development initiatives.

👁️

Our Vision

To foster comprehensive national development through widespread, accessible, and impactful community initiatives that uplift the underprivileged and build equal opportunity for every citizen.

🎯

Our Core Mission

  • Develop comprehensive support centers for underprivileged individuals, providing essential facilities and basic nutrition.
  • Establish and enhance educational institutions, offering world-class learning facilities and affordable, high-quality education.
  • Improve existing educational institutions by upgrading amenities, faculty support, and student learning aids.
  • Promote the importance of education among rural communities, fostering grassroots awareness and active parental engagement.
  • Establish free, out-of-school learning centers in rural areas, providing curriculum-based tutoring and soft skills development.
🛡️

Quality Policy & Transparency

Uncompromising commitment to integrity, financial auditing, and accountability.

24/7 Financial Transparency

Ranjidam Group Trust (RGT) maintains complete financial transparency by ensuring all income and expenditures are recorded and accessible online.

Internal Quality Audits

RGT conducts regular internal quality audits across all administrative, project, and departmental operations to ensure efficiency and non-profit integrity.

External Professional Audit

RGT engages independent external auditors (B. Arun Balaji & Associates) to perform periodic statutory audits and maintain 80G/12AA compliance.
