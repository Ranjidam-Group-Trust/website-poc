Teaching Volunteer

"Contribute your knowledge to make a perfect citizen for this country." Join RGT as a teaching volunteer to mentor children in orphanages and rural government schools.

Structured Teaching Framework

How Teaching Volunteering Works

As an RGT Teaching Volunteer, you will teach children residing in orphanages, shelter homes, and rural government schools around Chennai and Veerapandi.

Curated Curriculum: You do not need to prepare content from scratch! RGT provides structured teaching modules, worksheets, and value-added lesson plans under our Iragugal (Free Education) and Gnanam (Science Awareness) initiatives.

  • Basic Mathematics, English Communication & Science
  • Value Education, Moral Stories & Life Skills
  • GATE exam and competitive exam guidance for senior students
  • Art, Public Speaking & Talent Mentoring (Varnangal)

✨ Volunteer Benefits

📜 Certificate of Recognition

Official RGT Volunteer Service Certificate recognizing your teaching hours.

🕒 Flexible Scheduling

Weekend classes (Saturday/Sunday) or weekday evening sessions (1-2 hours/week).

🤝 Community Network

Connect with passionate educators, engineering professionals, and student mentors.
