🏘️ Village Capacity Building

RGT One — Veerapandi Chapter

Lifting the socioeconomic background of rural communities through targeted knowledge transfer, free student tuition, and vocational tailoring empowerment.

Grassroots Outcomes

Veerapandi Chapter Achievements

A model chapter showcasing how localized focus creates multi-generational economic upliftment.

📚

School Events & Contests

Conducted 6 Varnangal drawing events and 4 Gnanam life-skill seminars across 5 different schools in and around Veerapandi, benefiting hundreds of students.

✏️

Free Tuition Classes

Initiated free evening tuition classes for interested students in Veerapandi to assist with academic subjects, homework, and exam preparation.

🧵

Vocational Tailoring Batches

Conducted 2 complete batches of vocational tailoring courses, certifying over 50 women. Empowering women with self-employment skills boosts family economic stability.

🤝

Women's Self-Help Groups

Partnered with existing women's self-help groups (SHGs) for capacity building, health awareness, and International Women's Day Magalir Mattum celebrations.
