🌿 Environmental & Healthcare Action

RGT Social Service Unit

Mobilizing youth and volunteers for public cleanliness, environmental reforestation through seed balls, and free medical & dental outreach camps.

🧹

C-Force (Clean Force)

"The clean person is not the one that runs away from dirt but one who takes time to tidy up a dirty environment."
C-Force selects abandoned or neglected public spaces to improve their cleanliness. We actively involve children and youngsters, fostering civic pride and environmental responsibility.

🌱

Seed Bomb & Reforestation

"It's necessary to tell people to stop cutting trees, but it's even better to start planting them."
Children roll seed balls containing native seeds, clay, and compost. Volunteers launch these seed bombs in barren target areas to restore ecological balance.

🩺

Free Medical & Dental Camps

Organized in orphanages and rural villages with qualified medical consultants (Dr. Satheesh Kumar, Dr. Monisha T). Providing free health checkups, dental screening, expert advice, and essential medications at zero cost.
