Kid Sponsorship Drive

1. Sponsor a Gift to a Child

You can share any gift of your choice to brighten a child's day — whether it is stationery materials, school backpacks, notebooks, accessories, toys, storybooks, or educational kits.

Mass gift distributions are conducted through our flagship event drive titled "SMILE OVER MILES". RGT organizes the event and delivers your sponsored gifts directly to children in orphanages and rural government schools.

  • Stationery Sets (Pens, Notebooks, Geometry boxes, Crayons)
  • School Uniforms & Footwear
  • Educational Toys, Puzzles & Books
  • Personal Care & Hygiene Kits for young girls

Smile Over Miles Impact

Eco-Friendly Charity Initiative

2. Eco Return Gifts for Family Occasions

Make your family celebrations green and meaningful. Purchase handmade Seed Balls, Plant Saplings, and Decorative Plant Pots from RGT as return gifts for your guests!

🌱

Organic Seed Balls

Handcrafted by RGT volunteers containing native tree seeds (Neem, Banyan, Tamarind, Peepal) enclosed in nutrient-rich soil. Perfect for weddings, birthdays, and corporate events.

🪴

Plant Saplings & Pots

Potted indoor and outdoor medicinal/flowering plant saplings packaged neatly with eco-friendly wrappers as memorable return gifts for your guests.

💚

100% Proceeds to Charity

We charge nominal, feasible rates. Beyond raw manufacturing cost, all remaining proceeds directly fund our educational scholarships and food security drives!
