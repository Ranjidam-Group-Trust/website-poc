Literacy & Merit Support

RGT Scholarship Scheme

The core objective of the RGT Education Development Unit is to promote literacy and encourage outstandingly meritorious and deserving students to pursue higher academic excellence.

We select candidates who feel higher education is an unattainable dream due to financial hardship. After thorough verification of academic records and socioeconomic background, RGT sponsors their education.

  • Comprehensive higher education fee sponsorship
  • GATE exam preparatory books & study materials
  • Free academic tutoring and career mentoring
