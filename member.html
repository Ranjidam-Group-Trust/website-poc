🌟 Be a Member — Be a Part of Success

RGT Donating Membership

"A single member is responsible for huge growth." Become a contributing member of Ranjidam Group Trust to sustain our free education, food security, and community programs month after month.

Sustained Social Impact

Why Join as an RGT Member?

Recurring membership contributions provide RGT with predictable, continuous funding to sponsor deserving students' college fees and conduct weekly food drives.

🎓

Direct Student Sponsorship

Your monthly contribution directly sponsors merit scholarships, textbooks, and competitive exam preparation materials for underprivileged students.

📊

Transparent Regular Updates

Receive quarterly progress reports, event photo updates, financial statements, and event invitations via email, SMS, and WhatsApp.

🧾

Tax Exemption (80G)

All member donations receive official tax deduction receipts under Section 80G and 12AA of the Income Tax Act of India.

Flexible Contribution Options

Membership Schemes

Choose a contribution schedule that fits your budget. Minimum contribution is ₹600 for 3 months (₹200/month).

How to Register as an RGT Member

1️⃣

Fill Digital Form or Download PDF

Fill out the digital membership form online via Google Forms, or download the printable PDF registration form.

2️⃣

Make Payment & Send Details

Transfer your membership fee via HDFC Bank Transfer or UPI (contact@rgforhelp.org.in) and email your receipt & PAN card details to contact@rgforhelp.org.in.

3️⃣

Receive Tax Receipt & Member Kit

Our team issues your 80G tax receipt, welcome letter, and adds you to the official RGT Donating Member Network.
