RGT Chapters

Empowering rural and semi-urban communities through tailored capacity building, vocational training, free student tutoring, and women's self-help groups.

Flagship Regional Chapter

Veerapandi Chapter (RGT-1)

The main objective of RGT1-Veerapandi chapter is to uplift the socio-economic background of rural residents through capacity building and knowledge transfer.

🎯

Chapter Objective & Mission

Veerapandi Chapter serves as our model village transformation hub. By partnering with local schools, youth clubs, and women's self-help groups, RGT delivers tailored educational, vocational, and healthcare support directly to villagers' doorsteps.

Explore RGT-1 Details ↗

✨ Key Chapter Achievements & Outcomes

  • Education Scheme: Sponsored higher education for 6 meritorious students.
  • Varnangal Events: Conducted 4 talent hunt events across 5 surrounding schools.
  • Gnanam Workshops: Held 4 science & awareness workshops for rural children.
  • Free Evening Tuitions: Launched free daily after-school classes for village students.
  • Vocational Tailoring: Graduated 2 batches of tailoring courses benefiting 50+ women.
  • Magalir Mattum: Annual women's empowerment & health sessions on Women's Day.
  • C-Force Cleanliness: Volunteer drives keeping village streets and public spaces clean.
  • Self-Help Group Support: Capacity building & financial literacy for local women's SHGs.
Expansion Roadmap

Bringing RGT Chapters to Your Region

Our chapter framework is designed to be replicated in rural centers and tier-2 towns across Tamil Nadu and neighbouring regions.

📍

1. Local Need Identification

Surveying rural pockets to identify gaps in school infrastructure, student dropout rates, and women's vocational needs.

🤝

2. Volunteer Mobilization

Recruiting passionate local youth, teachers, and professionals as chapter leaders and subject mentors.

🚀

3. Sustainable Execution

Deploying RGT flagship programs (Iragugal, Varnangal, Tailoring, Medical Camps) with 100% financial transparency.
