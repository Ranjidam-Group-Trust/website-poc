GENERAL PROJECTS





​
​
Magalir Mattum
  • There is no tool for development more effective than the empowerment of women.
  • We conduct ‘Magalir Mattum’ purely for the women empowerment and we make the girls come out of their comfort zone by conducting various events, where they showcase their hidden talents.
  • We also arrange lectures on girl education, skill improvements, hygiene, health and also how to fight against harassments.
  • We also give them books and kits that helps them to break their boundaries and builds their skills.
Varnangal
  • Children draw to express what they are feeling and thinking.
  • To increase the imagination skills, cognitive process, fine motor and coordination skills and to embrace the creativity and confidence of the kids we conduct drawing contests in a specific theme regularly.
  • We also conduct essay-writing competitions to bring out their views in current scenarios.
  • And we select the winners and award them to boost up their competitive spirit.
Gnanam
  • We conduct interactive seminars for the kids to improve their resilience, innovation, creativity, curiosity, self-confidence, empathy, optimism etc.
  • Our aim is to develop their life skills, increase their pro-social behavior, decrease negative thoughts, increase the ability to plan ahead and choose effective solutions to problems that they face in day-day life.
  • The kids also get to know about various career options through our career-guidance program.
  • We also have well trained volunteers to conduct various awareness programs.
Iragugal
  • This program was started to give customized lessons for kids as per their need and interest.
  • We have talented and well-trained volunteers to give one-to-one teaching, more time and attention to kids in flexible and convenient timings.
  • Through these students get to learn in smaller groups and in one-one basis. This is certainly advantageous to timid students as they get the opportunity to speak up and ask questions.
  • We train them in various subjects like Spoken English, Grammar, Hindi, Computer science etc.
​
Payanam
  • Travel helps open our mind. Moreover, excursions give exciting experiences deeper than the regular school environment affords. It helps kids to attain Socialization, Exposure, Curiosity and Memory retention.
  • Hence, we have this theme ‘Payanam’. We take the kids and the home incharges for a one-day trip in a sophisticated private bus along with some energy drinks, healthy snacks and yummy food.
  • We choose exciting educational places for the kids to experience new environment and enjoy a day away from their daily routine.
Ubasaripu​​
  • Be the reason someone smiles today!
  • Celebrate your big days with our kids and experience the magical bliss.
  • We have organized various birthday and anniversary celebrations and we are proud about the heart-warming moments we have shared with the kids and the donors.
  • We have 3 categories in food selection.
  • Budget Meal – Where food menu is made according to the donor’s budget.
  • Exotic Meal - Where food menu includes starters, main course and dessert.
  • Customized Meal – Where food menu is decided by the donors which might include nuts, Fruits, health drinks, Biscuits etc.
Smile Over Miles
  • Giving is caring and Caring is loving.
  • When you give, it always come back to you. Giving is like sowing seeds that will reap an abundant harvest in your life.
  • We all would have experienced the joy of receiving/buying what we wish.
  • But have we ever thought about the underprivileged kids? Don’t they have wishes?
  • Kids’ world is small and their wishes are also cute like them.
  • So we have a special program ‘Smiles Over Miles’, where we take notes of each and every  kid’s long time wish and fulfill it bringing a wide smile in their face.
  • We take utmost care in selecting the gifts exactly according to kid’s specifications, color, print and brand.
  • The day just doesn’t get end with the gifts! Cultural performances and yummy food are also part of the big day.
  • Even donors can choose products from the list to gift the kids and be a part of our celebration.​
Yoga Camp
  • Yoga helps in the development of physical, mental, social and spiritual health.
  • When the body is physically healthy, the mind is clear, focused and stress is under control.
  • Yoga camp was started to set up a healthy foundation, to boost the self-esteem and to bring confidence for kids. We conduct regular yoga camps with trained professionals to teach them to persevere, be patient, be mindful, be generous, be focused and to work towards their goals. We work with the kids to improve their strength and flexibility.
Pongal - O – Pongal
  • The main objectives of the program is to celebrate ‘Samathuva Pongal’ with the children of orphan home.
  • From breakfast to dinner, we cook together with the kids and celebrate our Tamil festival. Pongal is incomplete without Rangolis. Hence, we conduct Rangoli events and other traditional events like ‘Uriyadi’, Kabadi etc.
  • We also take our kids for a short trip in tractor and bullock cart to make it more traditional.

Puradham (p++)
  • Protein is an important ingredient in the entire living cell’s survival. It helps in building, maintaining and repairing body tissues.
  • Do you know that a school-age kid requires 19-32 grams of protein per day?
  • It is likely that about one third of rural population is at risk of not meeting their requirements.
  • Hence, to ensure that every kid deserves the protein, we released P++ program, where we offer protein rich foods to the kids.
  • We not only concentrate on protein, but we work on required nutrition according to the age, deficiency and season.​
​Hands for Humanity (H4H)
  • What could be nobler than spending time with kid at an orphanage, right? No matter how grouchy you’re feeling, the smile that greets you when you enter their home, heals everything.
  • To experience such divinity, we have a special program ‘Hands for Humanity’ where our passionate volunteers serve the food to the kids, spreading kindness. Because Kindness is the best form of humanity. Even donors are allowed to participate in this noble program.​

​
Deepavali Oligal
  • The true happiness hidden in the Kids’ faces outweigh the pleasures of materialistic possession.
  • We celebrate Diwali with the Kids of homes that don’t receive much help and are in outskirts to experience the real happiness.
  • We buy them sweets, giving them happy moments with crackers and providing them good food for a day.
  • We share our happiness on Diwali not only with our family members but also with non-family members.
  • The excitement on the faces of the kids of the orphanage brings us the sense of relief.​
RWR (Run with RG)
  • Marathons are a healthy way to promote any cause. Its own motto is “to run for keeping oneself healthy”.
  • We conduct free marathons for kids and run with a message or cause.
                                            "Let the sweat smoothen our humanity."
​
Manavar Kalam
  • As we all know that children are the future of any country, the values and habits taught to them not only reflects their future but also reflects the future progress of the country.
  • Children should be taught politics in school so that they get better understanding of all the proceedings. Political learning helps them to widen their outlook.
  • Hence, we have ‘Manavar Kalam’ where the children are encouraged to develop their political knowledge in a more practical way thus becoming a responsible citizen for society
