RANJIDAM GROUP TRUST SOCIAL SERVICE UNIT

C-Force (Clean Force)
  • The clean person is not the one that runs away from dirt but one who takes the time and efforts to tidy-up a dirty environment.
  • Cleaning plays a vital role in our daily lives. Hence we choose public places to improve the appearance of that area that was previously neglected or abandoned.
  • The main motto of our Force is to involve our future generation of society in to this Cleanliness program.
  • Yes!!! We are so proud that we do this program with an active array of Kids and youngsters.
Seed Bomb
  • Seed bombs are little nuggets of clay, compost and native seeds that you throw at the ground.
  • Seed bombing has been actively practiced by our trust for reviving our green earth. Because “It’s necessary to tell people to stop cutting trees, but it’s even better to start planting them.”
  • We give the little revolution in the palm of our kids’ hands. Yes!!! Our kids roll  out the constructive bombs with their teeny tiny hands. Thus, the tender hands take part in bringing back the lost ecosystem balance.
  • And our volunteers launch them in the desired areas.

Medical Camp
  • Free medical camps are set up with a sacred aim to bring awareness among underprivileged people, who have no access to basic health care services or knowledge about the health care.
  • We conduct medical camps to provide free medical advice, medicine to the unfortunate people and refer for specialized treatment whenever it is required.
  • We have a set of talented doctors and health care providers who makes sure that orphan people and kids are getting the healthcare at the right time. we also provide all necessary medications free of cost.
