Sustainable Educational Growth

About RGT RISE Foundation

RGT RISE is an essential foundation wing operating under the umbrella of Ranjidam Group Trust (RGT), dedicated to providing quality free education to underprivileged and needy children.

With a strong commitment to fostering long-term educational opportunities, RGT RISE operates on a corpus fund model. By collecting and managing an endowed corpus fund, RGT RISE ensures sustainable institutional growth, enabling the realization of permanent educational campuses, vocational centers, and learning communities.

  • Endowed Corpus Fund Management for long-term sustainability
  • Establishing free schools and educational institutions
  • Setting up vocational training & skill workshop centers
  • Promoting holistic development and youth self-reliance

💡 Corpus Model Highlights

1. Perpetual Support

Invested principal generates yield to fund education programs indefinitely.

2. Institutional Infrastructure

Direct funding for physical classrooms, digital labs, and workshop tools.

3. 100% Tax Exempt

All contributions to RGT RISE receive 80G and 12AA tax deduction receipts.

Multifaceted Strategy

Pillars of the RGT RISE Vision

A comprehensive approach to enhancing education, vocational skills, and community empowerment.

🏫

1. Schools & Institutions

Building primary, secondary, and higher education institutions to provide accessible, high-quality education as foundational platforms for children's learning.

🛠️

2. Vocational Training Centers

Equipping youth and adults with practical, market-demanded skills to reduce unemployment and improve economic stability for underprivileged families.

🔬

3. Workshop Centers

Facilitating hands-on learning experiences in various trades, technology, and handicrafts to foster innovation and practical craftsmanship among learners.

👥

4. Community Engagement

Involving local leaders and families in program implementation, ensuring educational initiatives address specific socio-cultural needs.

🌱

5. Holistic Development

Combining academic learning with life skills, ethical values, and critical thinking to prepare youth to face real-world challenges confidently.

♻️

6. Long-term Sustainability

Establishing a self-sustaining network of facilities so future generations continue to benefit from expanding educational opportunities.
