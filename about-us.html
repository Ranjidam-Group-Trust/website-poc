Driven by Purpose, Strengthened by Resilience

Ranjidam Group Trust (RGT) is the dedicated charitable arm of Ranjidam Group, established by a passionate group of friends to empower underprivileged communities through education and food security.

Identity & Purpose

Who We Are

Ranjidam Group Trust (RGT) was founded on December 11, 2010 by a group of like-minded friends who shared a common vision: creating a fairer, more compassionate society where every individual has access to fundamental rights.

RGT operates as an independent non-profit trust registered under the Indian Trust Act (Registration No. 677/2011) and holds 80G & 12AA tax-exempt status granted by the Income Tax Department of India.

Core Vision & Principles

🎓 Education as a Tool for Change

Bridging the gap between deserving students and quality education, particularly in rural regions where access is severely restricted.

🍲 Sustaining Basic Needs

Providing consistent food support to individuals and families in need, recognizing that effective learning requires a healthy, nourished body.

💪 Overcoming Challenges

While initial societal and logistical obstacles delayed early progress, these challenges ultimately strengthened RGT's resolve and clarified our long-term vision.

Governance & Integrity

Board of Trustees & Operations

RGT is governed by a dedicated board of trustees ensuring complete financial transparency, ethical operations, and maximum community impact.

🏛️

Legal Trust Compliance

Fully compliant with all Indian statutory requirements for public charitable trusts. Annual audits and tax filings ensure complete transparency.

💼

100% Volunteer Leadership

Our board and team operate on a voluntary basis, ensuring that every rupee donated goes directly toward beneficiary welfare and project execution.

📍

Rooted in Chennai

Headquartered in Kathirvedu, Chennai, RGT extends its educational and food support programs across surrounding rural and semi-urban communities.
