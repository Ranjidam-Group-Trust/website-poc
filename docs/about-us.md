### 🌟 **Ranjidam Group Trust (RGT) — About Us**

#### **Identity & Purpose**
- **RGT is the charitable arm of the Ranjidam Group**, created to serve underprivileged communities.
- Founded **on December 11, 2010** by a group of friends.
- Operates as a **non‑profit trust** registered under the Indian Trust Act (Registration No. 677/2011).
- Holds **80G and 12AA tax‑exempt status** from the Income Tax Department of India.

---

### 🎓 **Core Mission**
- RGT focuses on **education as a tool for social change**.
- Works to **bridge the gap between deserving students and quality education**, especially in rural areas.
- Believes that an educated population can help reduce social issues and support socioeconomic development.

---

### 🍲 **Support for Basic Needs**
- Alongside education, RGT provides **food support** to individuals and families in need.
- Emphasizes that meeting basic needs is essential for effective learning and growth.

---

### 💪 **Journey & Challenges**
- Early challenges—including **societal corruption**—delayed progress.
- These obstacles ultimately **strengthened the organization’s resolve** and clarified its long‑term vision.
